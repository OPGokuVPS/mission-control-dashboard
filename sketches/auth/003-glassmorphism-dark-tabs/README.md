## Variant 003 — Glassmorphism Dark Tabs

### Design stance
Premium dark aesthetic with glass-morphism card, tabbed auth switching (Social/Email), and animated mesh background — conveys cutting-edge technology feel.

### Key choices
- Layout: single-column centered on rich animated gradient mesh background
- Auth UX: tab switcher (Social / Email) inside a frosted glass card — lets users pick their preferred method without scrolling past stuff they don't want
- Background: deep navy with subtle radial gradients + floating particles for atmosphere
- Card: rgba glass effect with backdrop-blur, subtle border, inner glow
- Button: multi-color gradient border glow animation on hover (purple → pink → indigo rotation)
- Typography: uppercase tracking-wide for brand name, smaller text scale overall
- Color palette: near-black bg, white text at reduced opacity, purple/indigo accents

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: visually striking, clearly different from generic templates, interactive tabs reduce cognitive load
- Weak at: can feel "too much" for an internal tool, glass effects may not render consistently across browsers, particles add visual noise

### Best for
- Consumer-facing or externally-hosted tools where first impression matters
- Teams that want the dashboard to feel like a premium SaaS product
